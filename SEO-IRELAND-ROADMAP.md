# Asovix — Irish SEO Roadmap

**Status: research and planning only. Nothing here has been published.**

Ireland is the beachhead market. Cork is where the business is based; Ireland is
the market it serves. This document lists the first 15 opportunities worth
investigating — it is not a content calendar, and no page below should be written
until proper keyword research has been done.

---

## ⚠️ On search volume

**No search-volume figures appear in this document, deliberately.** I have no
keyword-tool access, and inventing numbers would be worse than having none —
they would get used to prioritise real work.

Before writing anything, run these topics through Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs
or Semrush **with the location set to Ireland**, and record:

- Irish monthly search volume (not global, not UK — the difference is large)
- keyword difficulty
- what currently ranks on google.ie, and whether it is any good
- whether the SERP is dominated by IrishJobs, Jobs.ie, Indeed, GradIreland or
  university career-service pages — if so, a direct hit is likely unwinnable and
  the angle needs to change

Ireland is a small market. A topic with a few hundred Irish searches a month can
be commercially excellent if the intent is right. Do not dismiss low volume.

---

## The bar every page must clear

Do not build a generic careers blog. Every page should satisfy at least one:

1. Brings commercially relevant Irish job seekers
2. Solves a real job-search problem
3. Demonstrates Asovix expertise
4. Feeds qualified people into the diagnosis
5. Builds topical authority around career positioning

Qualified Irish traffic is the goal. Traffic on its own is not.

## A. HIGH PURCHASE INTENT
*People actively looking to pay someone for help.*

### 1. CV writing / CV help Ireland
- **Intent:** Commercial. Ready to hire someone.
- **Funnel stage:** Bottom
- **Page type:** Service page — `/cv/cv-help-ireland`
- **Why it matters:** The most direct commercial term in the market. Also the most
  contested, and the one where Asovix has to draw a clear line between a rewrite
  and positioning work — that distinction *is* the differentiator.
- **CTA:** Check my positioning
- **Priority:** High

### 2. CV optimisation / CV review Ireland
- **Intent:** Commercial, slightly earlier — wants an assessment first.
- **Funnel stage:** Bottom
- **Page type:** Service page + diagnosis entry
- **Why it matters:** "Review" intent maps almost perfectly onto the diagnosis. This
  is the cleanest fit between what people search and what we already built.
- **CTA:** Find out what's blocking you
- **Priority:** High

### 3. LinkedIn profile optimisation Ireland
- **Intent:** Commercial.
- **Funnel stage:** Bottom
- **Page type:** Service page — `/linkedin/linkedin-optimisation-ireland`
- **Why it matters:** Maps to a real €55 service, less contested than CV terms, and
  we hold a genuinely strong Irish proof point (a candidate observed an employer
  checking her LinkedIn before *and* after interview).
- **CTA:** Check my positioning
- **Priority:** High

### 4. Interview preparation / mock interview Ireland
- **Intent:** Commercial, often urgent — an interview is booked.
- **Funnel stage:** Bottom
- **Page type:** Service page — `/interviews/interview-preparation-ireland`
- **Why it matters:** Urgency converts. Maps to the €85 service. Someone with a date
  in the diary is the least price-sensitive buyer we have.
- **CTA:** Book interview preparation
- **Priority:** High

## B. PROBLEM AWARE
*Experiencing rejection. Do not yet know what to buy — or that this is buyable.*

### 5. Why am I not getting interviews
- **Intent:** Diagnostic. Frustrated, actively looking for an explanation.
- **Funnel stage:** Middle
- **Page type:** Long-form answer — `/job-search/why-am-i-not-getting-interviews`
- **Why it matters:** **This is the single best fit on the list.** It is the exact
  question the diagnosis answers, and the exact sentence in the homepage headline.
  The page should genuinely answer it, then offer the personalised version.
- **CTA:** Find out what's blocking you
- **Priority:** High

### 6. Applied to 100 jobs and no response Ireland
- **Intent:** Frustration, near-despair.
- **Funnel stage:** Middle
- **Page type:** Answer + evidence — `/job-search/applied-to-100-jobs-no-response`
- **Why it matters:** High volume of applications with no return is precisely the
  signal the diagnosis engine already scores as a positioning problem. The page can
  make that argument honestly, using the volume-versus-conversion framing.
- **CTA:** Diagnose my job search
- **Priority:** High

### 7. How many jobs should I apply for
- **Intent:** Informational, but reveals the same underlying anxiety.
- **Funnel stage:** Middle
- **Page type:** Answer — `/job-search/how-many-jobs-should-i-apply-for`
- **Why it matters:** Lets Asovix make its most counter-intuitive argument — five
  well-positioned applications beating fifty generic ones — which we can illustrate
  with a real candidate who applied to about five and had three interviews.
- **CTA:** Check my positioning
- **Priority:** Medium

### 8. Career change Ireland — how to start
- **Intent:** Exploratory, high anxiety, long consideration.
- **Funnel stage:** Middle
- **Page type:** Guide — `/career-change/career-change-ireland`
- **Why it matters:** Career switchers are one of the four named personas, and their
  core problem — experience that reads as belonging to someone else's career — is
  exactly what repositioning fixes.
- **CTA:** Find out what's blocking you
- **Priority:** Medium

## C. INFORMATIONAL
*Learning. Slower to convert, builds topical authority.*

### 9. How to tailor a CV to a job description
- **Intent:** Practical, task-focused.
- **Funnel stage:** Top/middle
- **Page type:** Tutorial — `/cv/how-to-tailor-cv-to-job-description`
- **Why it matters:** The mechanical version of "what employers are buying" — one
  third of the positioning equation. Strong internal-link hub to CV service pages.
- **CTA:** Check my positioning
- **Priority:** Medium

### 10. Irish CV format — what to include
- **Intent:** Informational, with a genuine local angle.
- **Funnel stage:** Top
- **Page type:** Guide — `/cv/irish-cv-format`
- **Why it matters:** A real Ireland-specific question (photo or not, referees,
  page length, PPS number) that international students and returning emigrants ask
  constantly. Naturally geographic without keyword-stuffing "Ireland".
- **CTA:** Check my positioning
- **Priority:** High

### 11. Graduate interview questions (Ireland)
- **Intent:** Preparing for a specific interview.
- **Funnel stage:** Middle
- **Page type:** Guide — `/interviews/graduate-interview-questions`
- **Why it matters:** High intent-adjacency to the €85 service. Asovix's angle —
  prepare a few pieces of evidence rather than fifty answers — is differentiated
  from the generic question lists that currently rank.
- **CTA:** Book interview preparation
- **Priority:** Medium

### 12. LinkedIn for students and graduates in Ireland
- **Intent:** Informational.
- **Funnel stage:** Top/middle
- **Page type:** Guide — `/linkedin/linkedin-for-graduates-ireland`
- **Why it matters:** Reaches students before the panic, and university career
  services may link to it — one of the few realistic Irish backlink sources, and it
  doubles as a warm route into the B2B pilot.
- **CTA:** Check my positioning
- **Priority:** Medium

## D. FREE TOOL INTENT
*Looking for a checker, scorer or assessment. Highest lead-capture potential.*

### 13. Free CV checker / CV score Ireland
- **Intent:** Wants an instant automated assessment.
- **Funnel stage:** Middle — very high lead capture
- **Page type:** Tool landing page pointing at `/diagnosis`
- **Why it matters:** **The diagnosis already is this tool.** This is the cheapest
  win available: an existing asset with search demand already pointed at it.
  ⚠️ Be careful with the promise — the diagnosis assesses *positioning* from answers,
  it does not score an uploaded CV out of 100. The page must describe what it really
  does, or it will convert well and disappoint immediately.
- **CTA:** Find out what's blocking you
- **Priority:** High

### 14. Am I applying for the right jobs? / career direction check
- **Intent:** Uncertain about targeting.
- **Funnel stage:** Middle
- **Page type:** Tool landing page pointing at `/diagnosis`
- **Why it matters:** Role targeting is one of the four signals the engine already
  scores, so a second, differently-framed entrance into the same tool — reaching
  people who do not think their CV is the problem.
- **CTA:** Diagnose my job search
- **Priority:** Medium

## E. ROLE-SPECIFIC
*Where programmatic SEO could eventually apply. Not yet.*

### 15. Graduate [role] CV Ireland — e.g. graduate sales, biomedical placement
- **Intent:** Specific, commercial, low competition individually.
- **Funnel stage:** Middle/bottom
- **Page type:** Role cluster — `/cv/graduate-sales-cv-ireland`
- **Why it matters:** Asovix has genuine role-level evidence — a graduate sales
  candidate and a biomedical engineering placement candidate, both real, both with
  permission. Start with the two roles where real evidence exists.
- **CTA:** Check my positioning
- **Priority:** Medium — **but write two by hand first**

> **Do not generate these programmatically yet.** Two hand-written pages for roles
> where Asovix holds real candidate evidence will teach more than fifty templated
> ones, and near-duplicate pages built by swapping a job title carry real risk of
> being treated as spam. Scale only once a hand-built page has proven it can rank.

## Suggested sequence

**Phase 1 — validate.** Keyword research on all 15 with location set to Ireland.
Kill anything where the Irish SERP is locked up by job boards.

**Phase 2 — build three.** #5 (why am I not getting interviews), #13 (free CV
checker), #10 (Irish CV format). These are the three where demand, differentiation
and an existing asset all line up.

**Phase 3 — measure.** GA4 is already wired to track `organic landing page →
diagnosis_started → diagnosis_completed → service_recommended → begin_checkout →
purchase`. Give it 8–12 weeks before judging anything.

**Phase 4 — expand.** Only then decide between more informational pages and the
role-specific cluster, based on what actually converted.

## Architecture notes

The site is ready for these clusters without further structural work:

- `pages/sitemap.xml.js` builds the sitemap from one `PAGES` array — add a route
  and it appears automatically.
- `lib/seo.js` exports a `<Seo>` component handling title, description, canonical,
  Open Graph, Twitter and JSON-LD, plus a `breadcrumbLd()` builder ready for nested
  cluster URLs.
- `components/DiagnosisCta.js` is the reusable end-of-article call to action, so
  every future page routes into the diagnosis the same way.
- Content clusters map to folders: `pages/cv/`, `pages/graduate-careers/`,
  `pages/interviews/`, `pages/linkedin/`, `pages/job-search/`, `pages/career-change/`.

A new page therefore needs: the file, one `<Seo>` block, one `<DiagnosisCta />`,
and one line in the sitemap's `PAGES` array.
